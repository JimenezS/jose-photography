Frequently Asked Questions

We focus entirely on natural-light outdoor portraiture that captures genuine connection, movement, and candid emotion. No stiff, awkward posing—just vibrant, sun-drenched environments and true-to-life skin tones.

Every booking includes a personalized styling and prompt-based posing guide. As a rule of thumb, neutral, earthy, or soft pastel tones complement natural outdoor backdrops and keep the focus timelessly on you.

Your professionally edited digital images are delivered securely via a password-protected Pixieset client portal. Turnaround time is typically 1 to 2 weeks for portraits and next-day for real estate listings.

South Florida weather can be unpredictable. We monitor forecasts closely leading up to your outdoor shoot and are always flexible to seamlessly reschedule or pivot to a covered architectural backup location at no extra charge.
